# Bibliography

Full BibTeX entries are in `references.bib`. This is an annotated guide to where each topic is standard.

- Weinberg, Gravitation and Cosmology (1972) and Cosmology (2008): perturbation theory, SVT decomposition, gauge transformations.
- Bardeen, Phys. Rev. D 22, 1882 (1980): gauge-invariant cosmological perturbations.
- Kodama and Sasaki, Prog. Theor. Phys. Suppl. 78, 1 (1984): gauge-invariant formalism.
- Mukhanov, Feldman and Brandenberger, Phys. Rep. 215, 203 (1992): theory of cosmological perturbations and canonical variables.
- Mukhanov, Physical Foundations of Cosmology (2005).
- Grishchuk, Sov. Phys. JETP 40, 409 (1975): gravitational waves in an expanding universe.
- Starobinsky, JETP Lett. 30, 682 (1979): spectrum of relic gravitational radiation.
- Abbott and Deser, Nucl. Phys. B 195, 76 (1982): stability of gravity with a cosmological constant.
- Bunch and Davies, Proc. R. Soc. A 360, 117 (1978): de Sitter vacuum.
- Birrell and Davies, Quantum Fields in Curved Space (1982).
- Maldacena, JHEP 05 (2003) 013: non-Gaussian features of primordial fluctuations.
- Misner, Thorne and Wheeler, Gravitation (1973): sign conventions.

Entries should be checked against the originals before formal citation.
